Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Affiliate Pin Automation is a personal project developed to assist with creating and publishing affiliate product Pins to the developer's Pinterest Business account.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access Pinterest account information, boards and Pins needed for its publishing functionality.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to support the application's personal Pin creation and publishing features.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with other users. Pinterest processes information according to its own privacy policy.

Data Security

The developer will take reasonable steps to protect account authorization credentials and any information handled by the application.

Third-Party Services

The application may use Pinterest's official API and affiliate product links from third-party shopping platforms.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the application's Pinterest access through their Pinterest account settings.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through their GitHub profile.
